# Privacy Policy

This add-on does **not** collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data.

## Data access
- The add-on runs only on the Amazon pages listed in `manifest.json`.
- It reads page content and DOM elements **only** to locate your Subscribe & Save subscription cards and to click the relevant cancellation controls.

## Data storage
- No persistent storage is used.
- Any state (selected items, progress) is held in memory and cleared when the page is refreshed or closed.

## Network activity
- The add-on makes **no** network requests to third-party servers.
- It does not use analytics, telemetry, ads, tracking pixels, or remote code.
